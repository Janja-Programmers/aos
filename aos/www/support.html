Support

Welcome to Africa Online Stores ("we", "our", "us") Support. We are here to assist you with any issues, questions, or feedback related to our application and related services. By contacting support, you agree to provide accurate information so we can help you effectively.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
For All Users:
Download the Africa Online Stores mobile app from Play Store or App Store, or visit our website. On the login page, click “Don’t have an account? Sign up.” Fill in your details, choose your user type (Buyer or Vendor), agree to the terms, and click Sign Up.
To log in, you must have an account. Enter your registered email and password on the login page and click Login.
Click “Forgot Password?” on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your email to reset your password.
Go to Account Settings, select “Delete Account,” and follow the instructions. All personal data will be removed. Learn more.
For Vendors:
Go to “Products,” click “Create New,” fill in product details including photos, description, and price, then save.
Go to “Stock Intake” and click “Create New.” Fill in the product details, quantity, and valuation rate (optional). Use “Add Another Entry” if you need to add multiple products. Once done, save and submit the form to update the stock levels. If stock is not added for products with “Maintain Stock” enabled, buyers will see those items marked as “Out of Stock.”
Go to “Sales Orders” to view all orders from buyers. You can find their contact details and complete the sale outside the app.
Click the “Deliver” button on a Sales Order (mobile app) or “Create Delivery Note” on web. This updates stock quantities to reflect the delivery.
After creating a Delivery Note, click the “Bill” button to generate a Sales Invoice. This completes the transaction in the system.
For Buyers:
Browse for the products you want and view their details on the product detail page. From there, you can either contact the vendor directly to place an order, or add the items to your cart, provide your address, and complete the order through checkout.
On the product detail page, click the “Action” button and select “Report Product.” Choose a reason, add details, and submit. Our team will review it.
On the product detail page, click the “Action” button and select “Review Product.” Add a rating and comment, then submit.
